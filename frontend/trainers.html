How matching works

This prototype uses transparent rules to rank internal trainers.

01 · Skill expertise

Trainers must have a higher level in the selected skill than the employee.

02 · Availability

Available trainers receive a higher score than trainers with limited availability.

03 · Ranking

Results are ordered by match score, expertise and reported weekly session capacity.
